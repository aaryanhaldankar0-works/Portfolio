I design mobility. Occasionally it even moves.

Mobility and vehicle designer, and professional bridge between “that'll never work” and a finished product. I sit between feasibility and emotion — technical enough to know what's possible, human enough to care whether anyone actually likes it.

Tap one — I promise it's more interesting than a bullet list. The part where I sketch things that don't exist yet and convince people they should. The part where I find out why they actually don't exist yet, and fix that. Zooming out until one part's problem turns into everyone's problem — on purpose. Asking “would a real person want to sit in this” before anyone else does.
